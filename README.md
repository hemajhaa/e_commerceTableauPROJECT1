# 🛒 E-Commerce Business Performance Analysis

**Turning e-commerce data into business decisions.**

This Tableau project analyzes **sales, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and discount impact** to understand what is driving the business and where improvement opportunities exist.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The goal was to answer key business questions:

* How is revenue and profit performing over time?
* Which regions contribute the most profit?
* Which product categories and products perform best?
* How do discounts affect profitability?
* Where are the biggest opportunities for improvement?

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes:

* 💰 **Total Net Revenue**
* 📈 **Total Profit**
* 🛍️ **Total Orders**
* 📊 **Profit Margin %**
* 📅 **Monthly Revenue Trend**
* 🌎 **Regional Profit Analysis**
* 🏷️ **Sales by Category**
* 🏆 **Top 10 Products**
* 💸 **Impact of Discount on Profitability**

The analysis focuses on **delivered orders** to keep the performance metrics aligned with completed business transactions.

## 🔍 Business Analysis Approach

**Business Question → KPI → Trend → Comparison → Insight → Decision**

Rather than simply visualizing numbers, I used Tableau to identify patterns that could support decisions around **pricing, discounts, product strategy, and regional performance**.

A good dashboard doesn't just show **what happened** — it helps stakeholders understand **why it happened and where to act next**.
